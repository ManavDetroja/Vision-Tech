# Official CP PLUS product research

Verified locally on 2026-11-15. Scope: product catalogue only; no deployment, database migration, authentication change, or real enquiry submission.

19 of the 22 exact models have an identified official product page and a valid exact-model official PDF datasheet. 829 populated specification fields have individual official source references. 12 uncertain/conflicting fields remain null and display Not yet verified. This is field-scoped verification, not a statement that every possible specification is known. Warranty terms remain unverified for all models.

## Research method

Exact model codes were searched individually on the official domain. Exact official page headings and product tables were inspected; datasheets linked by those pages were downloaded to the temporary research workspace and parsed. All 19 PDFs returned HTTP 200, decoded as PDF, and contained their exact model codes. Relevant conflicting PDF table pages were also rendered and visually checked. Shared manual URLs are recorded as page-linked references; they were not treated as exact-model evidence. No standalone manufacturer product photographs were downloaded or extracted from the PDFs.

The other three plausible exact URLs returned HTTP 200 without an exact matching product heading. A successful HTTP response is not product verification. Exact-domain, PDF/manual/catalogue and discontinued/renamed-model searches did not establish authoritative matches. No similar variant was substituted and no discontinued status was inferred.

## Important factual corrections

- CP-SL06K is officially a solar panel kit with built-in battery, not a camera. The existing solar category is retained and its subcategory is Solar Panel Kits.
- The four NVR input counts are verified as 4, 8, 16 and 32.
- CP-ANW-HP4H2-N65 has 4 PoE + 2 uplink ports and a 65 W total budget. CP-ANW-HPU8H2-N12 has 8 PoE + 2 uplink ports and a 120 W budget. Ethernet ports are not camera channels.
- CP-UNC-DA41L3C-D-Q2 is not the requested CP-UNC-DA41L3C-Q2 and was not substituted.
- 4G two-way talk is qualified as half-duplex according to the exact datasheets.
- Certificates with mixed company/product scope were omitted from retailer claims. No dealer authorization, pricing, stock, ratings, reviews or warranty was invented.
