# CP PLUS Product Image Acquisition Options

**Research date:** 2026-11-12  
**Product examined:** CP-UNC-DA21L3C-Q  
**Project changes:** This report is the only project file created for this task. The website and product database were not changed. No restricted product image bytes were requested or downloaded.

## Summary

The CP PLUS product page is publicly accessible and its HTML exposes seven gallery image references for CP-UNC-DA21L3C-Q. Every URL uses `/prodassets/product/small/`, so the page points to small/thumbnail variants; it does not expose a confirmed original-resolution URL or image dimensions. CP PLUS `robots.txt` disallows `/prodassets/`, so those image bytes were not requested.

CP PLUS publishes a 2025–26 product brochure and product-specific manuals/datasheets through public pages. The site also has a corporate-logo download page. These are user-facing downloads, but no public statement granting independent retailers commercial reuse rights to product photos was found. The public Authorized Partner Portal terms say site materials are protected and use requires express authorization. The reliable next step is a written permission and asset request to CP PLUS/Aditya Infotech.

## Permission request channel and scope

Recommended first contact: the official [CP PLUS Contact Us form](https://web.cpplusworld.com/contact). It has a query type, model number, and query fields and lists India sales enquiries at **1800 102 6526**. Ask that the request be routed to the marketing/brand-asset or legal rights owner. Do not treat the sales number as an image license grant.

An Aditya Infotech [official press release](https://www.adityagroup.com/press-release/10048) lists **Anupam Sah, Sr. GM Marketing** as a media contact at `anupam_s@adityagroup.com`. This is a public corporate marketing contact and a reasonable secondary route for routing the request; the published press release does not itself say this contact handles licensing. No message was sent.

Request written confirmation covering:

- Permission to reproduce and publicly display CP PLUS product photographs on VisionTech Solutions™’ independent commercial retailer website.
- Permission to download and host the files locally, resize/crop/compress them for responsive display, and use them only for the named models and product listings.
- Whether an official high-resolution image pack exists and how to obtain it through an authorized channel.
- Exact approved model coverage, domain, territory, duration, attribution/brand rules, and any restrictions on editing or advertising use.
- Confirmation that CP PLUS/Aditya Infotech controls or is authorized to license the supplied photographs.
- Written confirmation that a distributor or partner-provided image pack may be republished on a third-party retailer site (if the materials are supplied through that route).

## Authorized distributor and partner alternatives

The [official CP PLUS contact page](https://web.cpplusworld.com/contact) identifies Global Brand Pvt. Ltd. and UCC under its “Authorized Distributors” section. That establishes those named businesses as CP PLUS-listed distributors on that page, but it does **not** establish that either offers image packs, that they serve VisionTech’s market, or that their assets carry third-party commercial reuse rights. No public distributor image pack with a commercial reuse grant was found.

The [CP Partner program page](https://www.adityagroup.com/cp-partner) describes a partner-only app with product, service, and marketing support/resources. Its [portal terms](https://partner.cppluscloud.com/termsandcondition) describe access for registered authorized dealers and restrict dissemination of portal material to third parties. This is a credible route to ask CP PLUS about approved assets if VisionTech is eligible, but it is not evidence that an image pack is available or licensed for a retailer website. Obtain the specific written rights before using any portal asset.

No third-party reseller or distributor was treated as an authorized source for downloadable commercial-use photographs unless CP PLUS itself listed it. No external reseller file was adopted as an image source.

## Recommended next step

Submit a permission and asset request through the official CP PLUS contact form with the 22-model list above, asking for an approved, downloadable image pack and explicit permission for local hosting and commercial product-page display on VisionTech’s domain. If VisionTech has registered authorized-dealer status, also ask the official CP Partner team whether an approved media pack is available and whether the portal terms allow this exact third-party website use. Wait for written authorization and an approved source before retrieving or integrating the image files.

**Current conclusion:** public product pages, brochures, manuals, and brand logos exist, but the research did not find a public product-photo pack or a general commercial reuse license. The seven gallery references are documented; no restricted product image bytes were fetched and no images are claimed as newly downloaded.
